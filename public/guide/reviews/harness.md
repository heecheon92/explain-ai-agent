## Verdict

Needs changes. The receipt story and arithmetic work, but the walkthrough turns features of 지민’s example into general rules and does not clearly distinguish an executable request from an ordinary reply.

## Reader pass

- Step 1 sets the problem without a definition. By step 3 I could say what would happen: 지민 gives the same request, but now the program around the LLM can see the folder and do something with it.
- The key moment is steps 4–5: the LLM’s note arrives while the folder stays put; only the next scene shows the harness acting. Step 6 makes the return trip click by comparing it with 지민 pasting the names in `llm`.
- I reread step 8 to reconcile making a folder *and* moving a photo with the counter going up only once. Step 9 made me look back and forth between the number of tools, the number of actions and the number of photos moved.
- The titles mostly tell the story on their own. Main prose mostly reads naturally in 해요체; the prior lesson already explains LLM and previews 하네스, and step 3 explains 도구 in context. The optional source panel is less accessible than the main text.
- The final stage stands on its own: the LLM and harness exchange writing, only the harness connects to the folder, and the counters and folder tag distinguish 2 actions from 1 sorted photo. The four takeaways give reusable rules, once their scope is corrected.
